Task 3: Map of Prices Across Dubai's Communities

Question & Answer

Question: Which community has the largest number of sales of units and villas, and how many sales is it? Is this visualization effective in finding this community? Why?

Which community has the largest number of sales of units and villas, and how many sales is it?
The community with the largest number of sales of units and villas is Al Barsha South Fourth (Community Number: 681), with a total of 3,925 sales within the analysis window (followed by Business Bay with 3,634 sales, and Marsa Dubai with 2,955 sales).

Is this visualization effective in finding this community? Why?
No, this map is not effective for finding this community.

  • The colour shows the wrong thing: The map colours communities by median price per square metre, not by number of sales. Sales only appear in the tooltip, so the busiest community looks the same as any other until someone hovers over it.
  • Big areas steal the attention: Large desert communities with very few sales take up most of the map, while busy urban ones like Al Barsha South Fourth are small and easy to miss.
  • It takes too much effort: The only way to find the top community is to hover over all 226 of them and remember the highest number.
  • Better options: A sorted horizontal bar chart would show Al Barsha South Fourth at the top straight away. A bubble map, with size for sales and colour for price, would show both at once.
